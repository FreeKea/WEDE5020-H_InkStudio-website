Project Overview:
This will be a website for a tattoo studio that will allow clients/users to make bookings, view the artists work history
and also allow clients to make enquiries.

Website Goals and Objectives:
I want to make bookings much easier for clients as they will be able to view availability and book appointments
via an availability calendar or booking form.
A gallery of images or a page of the artist’s previous work and tattoo designs will be available to view as well on one of the website pages.
Will be providing a brief history of the artist as clients can learn about his style,
experience and history in the tattoo industry.
My website will be about trust and trying my best to attract new clients
(especially people that doesn’t have social media) with a visually appealing website.
