About The Artist

Welcome to H_InkStudio. I am a solo tattoo artist passionate about creating unique and meaningful tattoos for every client.

I believe that every tattoo should have its own story. Whether you have a specific idea in mind or need help developing a design, I work closely with each client to create something personal and individual.

My Tattooing Journey

I began my tattooing journey in April 2023 and have continued developing my skills, creativity and tattooing style through experience and working with clients.

Every tattoo has given me an opportunity to learn, improve and create something that both myself and my clients can be proud of.

My Approach

My goal is to make every client feel comfortable and involved throughout the tattoo process. From the initial idea through to the finished tattoo, I take the time to understand what each client is looking for.

I focus on creating tattoos with attention to detail, precision and creativity while making sure the final design feels personal to the individual.

My Specialties

My specialties include, line work, micros, anime designs, black and grey, colour work and custom designs. I am always open to exploring new styles and techniques to create unique tattoos for my clients.
